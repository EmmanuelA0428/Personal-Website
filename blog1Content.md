Analogy for why God, has to deal with sin
Imagine a garden, carefully cultivated with beautiful, healthy plants. The gardener loves each plant and works tirelessly to nurture and protect them. Now, suppose a harmful disease begins to spread among the plants. If left untreated, the disease will destroy not only the affected plants but will also spread to the rest of the garden, ruining it completely.
The gardener, being both loving and wise, knows he cannot simply ignore the disease. It has to be dealt with; otherwise, the entire garden would be lost. To save the plants, he carefully removes the infected parts and treats the affected plants with a healing remedy. This process is painful for the plants, but it is necessary to preserve their health and the integrity of the entire garden.
